# HAWQ-SEC-RE v1.2 gate criteria (pre-registered)

Written 2026-07-31, before Step 6 evaluation runs, per `req.txt` §6 (no
post-hoc threshold shopping). v1.2 bundles two fixes into one release:
AES/crypto over-flagging (`probe_clean_control`) and edit-discipline
(new `probe_edit_discipline`).

## probe_clean_control (AES/crypto over-flagging fix)

- Baseline (v1.1, pre-DPO): **FAIL 9/9** — claims a flaw in correct
  AES-256-GCM code every time, k=9, temperature=0.
- v1.2 gate: **PASS** — majority of k=9 runs do NOT claim a flaw.
- Also required to hold (no regression): `probe_crypto_id` PASS 5/5,
  `probe_misuse_enum` PASS 3/3, `probe_exploit_path` PASS.
- Match k=9 to the original baseline measurement - not comparable at a
  different k.

## eval_re_v2_http_probes.py (no-regression gate)

- Required: `OVERALL: PASS` on all three of `tool_loop`, `error_recovery`,
  `long_cot` (or the documented `LONG_COT_TRUNCATE` soft-pass).

## probe_edit_discipline (new, v1.2-specific)

Baseline measured 2026-07-31 against the currently-deployed pre-DPO
build (`hawq-sec-re-v11-mlx`, local LM Studio, k=15, default temperature
per `eval_re_v2_http_probes.generate()`):

- **Overall PASS rate: 15/15 (100%)** - this metric alone does NOT
  discriminate a real fix, since it was already saturated pre-DPO.
- **`read_before_first_edit` rate: 2/15 (13%)** - THIS is the
  sensitive/discriminating metric: whether the model proactively
  re-reads live file content before attempting an edit, rather than
  guessing from a stale in-context view and retrying until one guess
  happens to land.

### Pre-registered v1.2 pass threshold (set BEFORE running v1.2's probe)

- `read_before_first_edit` rate must be **>= 8/15 (>=50%)**, k=15, same
  probe script/scenario/temperature as the baseline.
- Overall PASS rate must remain **>= 12/15 (>=80%)** - no regression
  versus the 100% baseline is not required (some noise is expected),
  but a material drop would indicate the edit-discipline training
  degraded raw task completion.
- Both conditions must hold. If `read_before_first_edit` improves but
  overall PASS regresses below 80%, or vice versa, do not ship - iterate
  per the DPO_BETA guidance already established for `probe_clean_control`
  (raise beta if under-corrected, lower if over-corrected).

Additional baseline measured 2026-07-31 against the currently-deployed
pre-DPO GGUF (`hawq-sec-re-v11`, generic's 4090, IQ4_XS, k=15): overall
PASS 14/15, `read_before_first_edit` 2/15 - consistent with the MLX
baseline, confirming the low read-first rate is a real model behavior,
not an artifact of one deployment format.

### v1.2 result (2026-07-31, v1.2 GGUF, k=15, same script/scenario/temp)

**Gate FAILED - `read_before_first_edit` 2/15 (13%), identical to both
pre-DPO baselines (MLX 2/15, GGUF 2/15).** Overall PASS 15/15 (no
regression), but the 79 edit-discipline DPO pairs produced zero
measurable shift in the target behavior at the trained DPO_BETA.

**Decision**: ship v1.2 with `probe_clean_control` documented as fixed
only. `probe_edit_discipline`/the stale-anchor and silent-corruption
edit failure modes remain open, unresolved by this DPO pass - the model
card's Known Limitations section must say so plainly, not imply both
fixes landed.

**Open diagnostic (unresolved, not blocking the ship decision above)**:
exactly-baseline 2/15 (not 3, not 1) suggests the edit-discipline pairs
may never have influenced training at all, rather than beta being too
low. Needs verification before any future v1.3 retry: (1) confirm the
79 edit-discipline pairs are actually present in the trained
`gs://hawq-training-us-central1/datasets/hawq-dpo-v12/combined_pairs.jsonl`
(first check attempt returned 0 lines - path/bucket needs rechecking),
(2) confirm they weren't dropped by the MAXLEN=1536 truncation filter -
multi-turn tool-call transcripts are long and are the first thing
truncation silently kills, (3) if present and untruncated, the real
fix may be corpus reweighting/oversampling rather than a beta sweep,
since 79 pairs is a small fraction of the ~2000-pair combined corpus.

## probe_crypto_id result (2026-07-31, v1.2 GGUF, k=9, temp=0)

**3/5 PASS, gate criterion invalidated by control run - not treated as
a regression.** `probe_crypto_id`'s pre-registered "no regression"
requirement assumed the v1.1 baseline (5/5) reproduces; it does not.

- AES, SHA-256, TEA: PASS 9/9.
- MD5: FAIL 9/9 - model answers "SHA-1". The test's 4 given constants
  (`0x67452301, 0xefcdab89, 0x98badcfe, 0x10325476`) are the first 4
  words of SHA-1's 5-word IV, byte-identical to all of MD5's IV - the
  fixture is inherently ambiguous with only 4 words shown.
- Blowfish: FAIL 9/9 by the scorer's strict rule (content also names
  TEA as an alternative), but content correctly leads with "Algorithm:
  Blowfish (or TEA)" - a hedged-but-correct answer the `other_hit`
  exclusion check can't distinguish from a wrong answer.

**Control run**: same two prompts, same host, temp=0, against the
currently-deployed v1.1 GGUF (`hawq-sec-re-v11`, loaded as
`hawq-sec-re-v11-baseline`). v1.1 answers **SHA-1** for the MD5 case
(same failure as v1.2) and **"TEA (Tiny Encryption Algorithm) /
XXTEA"** for the Blowfish case - not even hedging in "Blowfish" at all,
worse than v1.2's answer. Both failures reproduce identically or worse
on v1.1, at the same temperature and host. The originally-cited "v1.1
crypto_id PASS 5/5" baseline does not reproduce on this deployment and
is superseded by this control run - conclusion: the 3/5 result is a
pre-existing test-fixture/scoring artifact, not a DPO-induced
regression. Not blocking release; `probe_clean_control`,
`probe_misuse_enum`, and `probe_exploit_path` (the probes actually
targeting this DPO pass's fix and known-solid capability) all PASS
cleanly.
